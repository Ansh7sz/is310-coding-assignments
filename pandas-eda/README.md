# PublicationAndPopularityInTopNovels

In this notebook, I explore how the Goodreads Top Ranked Novels dataset and the New York Times bestseller dataset represent different kinds of literary popularity. Rather than focusing on author gender, I look at publication trends, genre concentration, Goodreads ratings, and repeated appearances on the NYT bestseller list.

My main questions are:
- Which publication decades appear most often in the top novels dataset?
- Which genres are most common among top-ranked novels?
- Do some genres tend to receive higher Goodreads ratings than others?
